This art piece depicts Kaoru Sakurayashiki, commonly known as Cherry Blossom, from the original anime series *SK8 the Infinity*. The series, which aired in 2021, focuses on underground skateboarding. Cherry Blossom is a highly skilled, elegant skater known for his distinctive pink hair and his advanced AI skateboard, Carla, which he controls via an earpiece. This specific illustration is fan art, created by an artist using the handle 'Ure', offering a vibrant and expressive interpretation of the character outside of official productions.
